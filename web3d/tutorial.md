# The table

The pack is dealt face up into **fans** of three cards, and the last fan holds one.

Only the **top** card of a fan can be moved. Moving it frees the card beneath.

Every card is face up, so you can plan ahead.

# The foundations

Each suit has a **foundation**, where cards go *home*. Start with the **ace**, then the 2, 3 and so on up to the king, in suit.

Only a top card can go home. Get all 52 home to win.

# Building

A top card can also move onto another fan's top card if it is the **same suit** and **one rank higher**. The 6♥ goes onto the 7♥.

One card moves at a time. This is how you reach the cards buried below.

# Redeal

When no other move is left, you may **redeal**. The cards not yet home are gathered and dealt again in threes. They are shuffled, unless the rules say *in order*.

The counter at the top shows how many redeals are left. Cards at home stay there.

# The draw

On the last deal, once, you may **draw** one buried card out of any fan. It must be played at once: home, or onto a top card it can build on.

# Kings fill

A fan has emptied. In this game a **king** may move into an empty fan. No other card may.
